## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a nocturnal observatory aesthetic tailored for curious minds, wildlife enthusiasts, and competitive learners. The product balances scientific authority with gaming dynamism. Interfaces evoke a night-safari telemetry system: mysterious, immersive, and biologically precise.

Visual style combines **Dark Modernism** with controlled **Bioluminescent Glassmorphism**. Dark slate-emerald canvas tones minimize ocular fatigue during extended quiz sessions, while vibrant neon emerald and cyan highlights signify interactability, correct telemetry, streaks, and biological classifications. The aesthetic rejects sterile tech minimalism in favor of lush, atmospheric depth with soft optical glows, crisp borders, and refined translucent paneling.

## Colors
The color architecture relies on a multi-tier dark slate-emerald baseline paired with vivid bioluminescent emitters:

- **Surface Scales**:
  - Base Ground: `#0B1315` (Deep Abyssal Slate)
  - Card & Container Base: `#111E22` (Dark Jungle Moss)
  - Elevated Container / Hover: `#18282D` (Submerged Slate Emerald)
  - Highest Overlay / Dialogs: `#1E343B`
- **Telemetry & Accents**:
  - **Primary (`#10B981`)**: Neon Emerald. Represents verified answers, active habitat states, mastery XP, and primary call-to-actions.
  - **Secondary (`#06B6D4`)**: Bioluminescent Cyan. Represents quiz telemetry, timer countdowns, taxonomic badges, and secondary insights.
  - **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`)**: Amber Glow. Reserved for streaks, rare species indicators, warning timers, and achievements.
  - **Negative / Alert (`#F43F5E`)**: Coral Red. Denotes missed answers and low session vitality.
- **Typography & Content**:
  - Text Primary: `#F1F5F9` (Crisp arctic slate for maximum contrast against deep backgrounds)
  - Text Secondary: `#94A3B8` (Muted mist for supporting metadata, descriptors, and secondary attributes)
  - Border Accents: `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)` for ambient containment and `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.35)` for focused states.

## Typography
The system unifies the structural geometric confidence of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines, stat displays, and tags with the crisp rendering of **Inter** for descriptions, scientific notes, and quiz answer options. 

Numerical figures in quizzes, timers, and animal stats should utilize tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent layout shifting during active countdowns. Taxonomic nomenclature (genus, species) must be rendered in italicized Inter body styles. All uppercase labels leverage deliberate positive tracking to maximize scanning speed against dark canvases.

## Layout & Spacing
A 12-column responsive fluid grid governs page structure with strict safe bounds:

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns, `2.5rem` outer margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Max-width container capped at `1280px` for optimal reading and interaction ergonomics.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns, `1.5rem` outer margins, `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (Under 768px)**: 4 columns, `1rem` outer margins, `1rem` gutters. Quiz option grids collapse from 2x2 matrix into a vertical 4-tier stack.

Spacing rhythm is anchored on an 8pt base unit. Component internal padding employs `space-sm` and `space-md` for compact telemetry tiles, while flagship wildlife cards and quiz question prompts adopt `space-lg` to `space-xl` to promote psychological focus.

## Elevation & Depth
Atmospheric depth is forged via chromatic surface elevation, backdrop filtration, and subtle bioluminescent illumination rather than murky drop shadows:

- **Surface Level 0 (Canvas)**: Solid `#0B1315`.
- **Surface Level 1 (Static Cards, Shelves)**: `#111E22` with a 1px border of `rgba(241, 245, 249, 0.06)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Modules, Quiz Options)**: `rgba(24, 40, 45, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and border `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Active Options)**: `rgba(30, 52, 59, 0.92)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and perimeter border `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.3)`.
- **Bioluminescent Glow Profiles**:
  - Focus/Active Glow: `0 0 24px -4px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`
  - Streak/Hot State Glow: `0 0 28px -2px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.4)`
  - Modal Floating Shadow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`

## Shapes
The shape language uses moderate roundedness (`0.5rem` default, `1rem` for `rounded-lg`, `1.5rem` for `rounded-xl`) to achieve a sleek, high-tech handheld console profile. 

- Large cards, specimen feature windows, and quiz containers use `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem`).
- Interactive option tiles, buttons, inputs, and flyouts use `rounded-lg` (`1rem`).
- Badges, status pills, conservation metrics, and avatar rings utilize full organic pill geometry (`9999px`).
- Internal media and embedded animal illustrations inside cards must carry matching concentric radii (`calc(1.5rem - 0.5rem) = 1rem`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Quiz Confirm, Start Session)**: Solid `#10B981` background, `#0B1315` ultra-dark bold text, `rounded-lg`. Hover evokes an emerald glow (`0 0 20px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.45)`) and slight brightness lift.
- **Secondary Action (Lifelines, Hints, Secondary Discovery)**: Translucent surface `#18282D`, `#06B6D4` border and text. On hover, background shifts to `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.15)`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Transparent with `#94A3B8` text, transitioning to `#F1F5F9` with subtle background tint on hover.

### Quiz Choice Cards
- Default state: Translucent container `#111E22`, 1px border `rgba(241, 245, 249, 0.08)`, text `#F1F5F9`.
- Hover state: Border turns `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.5)`, background shifts to `#18282D`.
- Selected state: Border 2px `#06B6D4`, background `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.12)`, Cyan ambient glow.
- Correct Reveal: Solid border `#10B981`, background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`, Emerald pulse glow.
- Incorrect Reveal: Solid border `#F43F5E`, background `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.15)`.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- Conservation Status (e.g., "Critically Endangered"): Background `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.15)`, text `#F43F5E`, border `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.3)`.
- Habitat / Class Badges (e.g., "Mammalia", "Nocturnal"): Background `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.1)`, text `#06B6D4`, border `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.2)`.

### Input Fields & Search
- Background `#111E22` with inner subtle shadow.
- Border 1px `rgba(241, 245, 249, 0.1)`. Placeholder text `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state: Border shifts to `#10B981`, zero harsh browser outlines, soft outer glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`.

### Telemetry Timers & Streak Trackers
- Circular or linear progress tracks using a deep track background (`#18282D`) and glowing neon fills (`#10B981` transitioning to `#F59E0B` when under 5 seconds).
- Numerical displays formatted in tabular Plus Jakarta Sans bold.

### Specimen Knowledge Cards
- Multi-layered card featuring high-resolution animal media masked with top-rounded corners, seamlessly transitioning to a `#111E22` content body via an atmospheric gradient overlay (`from-transparent to-[#111E22]`).
- Includes quick-stat tags, acoustic spectrogram / call preview button, and difficulty indicator dots.
